# Research Status

**Last updated:** 2026-10-06

## Completed / documented

- Defined the initial sensing problem.
- Studied the idea of using existing wireless signals as opportunistic sensing sources.
- Studied the relationship between human movement and RF channel variation.
- Defined the role of CSI in the proposed signal-processing pipeline.
- Studied the role of an electronically reconfigurable receiving antenna.
- Defined the initial beam-scanning concept.
- Defined the closed-loop measurement and antenna-selection concept.
- Identified major false-positive sources.
- Defined a first experimental sequence.
- Listed candidate system and antenna performance metrics.
- Considered Raspberry Pi as the high-level processing and control platform.

## Not completed yet

- Final antenna topology
- Final operating frequency
- Hardware receiver/CSI acquisition setup
- Antenna control circuitry
- Validated signal-processing algorithm
- Experimental CSI dataset
- Measured antenna radiation patterns
- Detection/localization accuracy
- Final automotive application pairing

## Main research question

Can RF/CSI variations associated with human activity be distinguished from ordinary environmental RF variations, and can measurements across reconfigurable antenna states be used to identify a more promising spatial region?

## Current caution

The project should not claim reliable survivor detection or respiration detection until those capabilities are demonstrated experimentally.
