# Project Background

## IEEE AP-S design requirement

The 2026 IEEE AP-S Student Design Contest asks teams to design a compact, multifunctional antenna system for automotive applications and demonstrate its utility. The demonstration must cover at least two automotive applications involving communication and/or sensing.

The contest also emphasizes real-time visualization, ease of reproduction, clear explanations for non-specialists, and step-by-step teaching instructions.

## Problem being investigated

The research direction started from a disaster-rescue problem: locating people who may be trapped under rubble when visual or acoustic methods are difficult.

The idea is not to replace conventional rescue methods. The intended role is to provide an additional indication that can help a rescue system decide where to inspect first.

## RF sensing idea

A smartphone or another wireless device can act as an opportunistic RF source. Human movement can change the propagation environment and therefore affect measurable RF channel characteristics.

The research question is whether these changes can be extracted from RF/CSI measurements and combined with spatial information from a reconfigurable receiving antenna.

## Current framing

The underlying sensing technology is being considered in an automotive/rescue-platform context so that it can be developed within the AP-S contest requirements.

At this stage, the exact second automotive functionality and final demonstration architecture still need to be finalized.
